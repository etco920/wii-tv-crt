# Pointer artwork attribution

The unmodified `wii_pointer_sheet.png` is sourced from Crayon's Wii-Tac-Toe project:

https://github.com/Crayon2000/Wii-Tac-Toe/blob/master/gfx/hands.png

Only the first 96 × 96 player-one frame is drawn by this launcher, including the number in its palm. The sheet is cropped at draw time, not modified on disk. Cursor angle, anchoring, shadow rendering, CRT texture and menu theme are launcher code.

The upstream/fork MIT code license does not imply an additional license grant for third-party artwork. Original artwork rights and attribution remain with their respective authors. This is a fan interface, not an official Nintendo product.

The upstream audio and font credits remain in LICENSE, launcher/FONT-LICENSE-OFL.txt and tools/sounds/README.md.
